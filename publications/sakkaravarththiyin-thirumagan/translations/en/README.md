## Established baseline after Articles 1–8

Publication-wide:

- `ஆச்சாரியார்` → **Achariyar**; referent Rajaji remains outside Kalaignar's body in translator/editorial identification.
- Preserve source-label changes: explicit `இராஜாஜி` → **Rajaji**.
- Preserve commands, rhetorical questions, repetition, sarcasm, ridicule, physical imagery, source-supported wordplay and harsh formulations.
- Do not externally identify unexplained source-specific forms unless explicitly asked.

Article 8 adds:

- `நாடாண்ட மன்னன் நாதியற்று செத்தான்` → **The King Who Ruled the Land Died with No One to Tend Him**;
- `நாதியற்று / நாதியில்லாமல்` → **with no one to tend him**, preserving title/body irony;
- `தாம் பத்தினி` → contextual **devoted wife**, while preserving the source form in the translator note;
- scan-52 unmatched opening quotation is explicitly documented rather than silently closed;
- `சயந்தன்` → **Sayantan**, `பாக்கை` → **paakkai**, `சூதமாகதர்கள்` → **Suta-Magadhars** as source-bearing forms;
- `புலன்களை அடக்கியாளும் திறமை` → **ability to master his senses**, repeatedly retained as sarcasm;
- `காம போதை` → **intoxication of lust**, `உடல் சுகம்` → **bodily pleasure**;
- `வாலை அடக்கிப்` retains the physical **tail** image;
- `பூணூல் வேந்தர்` → **sacred-thread king**.
