# Living English Translation Lexicon — சக்கரவர்த்தியின் திருமகன்

This is the working consistency ledger for the English layer. It does not replace reading each Tamil sentence in context.

Permanent policy: [`../../../../ESSAY_TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md`](../../../../ESSAY_TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md)

## Rules

1. Preserve Kalaignar's source label, rhetorical function and emotional force.
2. Do not silently identify unexplained source-specific forms from memory or outside knowledge.
3. Contextual variants are allowed when the Tamil sense changes; record them.
4. Harsh, caste-polemical, religious, political and sexualised vocabulary must not be politely neutralised.
5. If a Tamil source anomaly matters to meaning, document it rather than repairing it in English.

## Publication-wide identity / label rule

The user explicitly established that `ஆச்சாரியார்` in this publication refers to **C. Rajagopalachari (Rajaji)** and chose **`Achariyar`** as the English body form.

- Articles **1–12** use **Achariyar** wherever the Tamil source uses `ஆச்சாரியார்`.
- Articles **13–14** must do the same unless Kalaignar himself changes label.
- If the source explicitly says `இராஜாஜி`, preserve **Rajaji**; Article 7 demonstrates this rule.
- Do not mechanically replace `ஆச்சாரியார்` with `Rajaji` in Kalaignar's body text.

## Article 10 contextual decisions

- Title `விஷ்ணு அவதாரம் எனப்படும் ராமனிடம்!` → **`To Rama, Who Is Said to Be Vishnu's Incarnation!`**.
- Heading/contents `எனப்படும் ... ராமனிடம்` and scan-63 body `என்பதும் ... ராமனிடமே` remain distinct source witnesses; do not harmonise them.
- All four page-boundary comments retained: scans **61–64 / printed pp.59–62**.
- `ஆச்சாரியார்` remains **Achariyar** throughout.
- The opening **Ramayana tigers** accusation and 69/41/42–69 accounting remain direct.
- Vatapi–Ilvala meat story retains **shraddha feast**, goat meat, **mesha (goat) meat**, **human flesh**, **Rakshasa flesh** and the challenge to rishis who **knew all three times**.
- Scan 63's unmatched opening parenthesis before `முரண்பாட்டைக் கவனியுங்கள்` is preserved and documented.
- The contradiction line is sharpened as **Now he has begun muddling it all up!** rather than a passive description of confusion.
- Surpanakha sequence retains **thirst of lust**, the odd source phrase **Two wives will give rise to business trouble**, **second wife**, and Rama's love surging toward her.
- `புருஷரத்தினங்கள்` → **gems among men**; `ரசாபாச` remains **rasabhasa**; `சப்பைக் கட்டு` reuses **splint**.
- Final nested quotation irregularities are documented; **Go to elder brother / Go to younger brother!** and the closing **Aryan culture** jab remain direct.

## Article 11 contextual decisions

- Title `நடப்பதெல்லாம் நாராயணன் செயலா?` → **`Is Everything That Happens Narayana's Doing?`**. The colloquial **doing** keeps the title's accusatory question rather than turning it into an abstract theological heading.
- All six page-boundary comments retained: scans **65–70 / printed pp.63–68**.
- `ஆச்சாரியார்` remains **Achariyar** throughout; closing source plural `ஆச்சாரியார்களுக்கு` remains **the Achariyars**.
- May **1954** versus May **1955** contradiction is retained, together with **backing orchestra**, **A disorder of the brain?—confusion?**, mire/foot image, **Chanakya**, and the direct **Brahmin leader** label.
- `நாராயணனோ தானே ராமன்...` remains the compact contradiction: **Narayana knows that he himself is Rama. But Rama does not think that he himself is Narayana.**
