# Not Here! In Russia

<!-- Tamil source: scan 30 / printed numeral not visible -->

Kakkaipadiniyar  
Nachellaiyar  
Kavarpendu  
Pari Magalir  
Perungop Pendu  
Vennikkuyaththiyar  
Veripadiya Kamak Kanniyar

Not new names! Creators of many treasures lying embedded in the Purananuru. Women! A band of women found on a page or two in the history of today's Dravidam, filled with grandmothers who sing the lullaby, “What need have stove-blowing women for education?”...... and with hidebound reactionaries!

Queens of Tamil who poured down a rain of poetry! Golden vines that gave Tamil a distinction all its own! Great women who seated the young bulls on the pedestal of valour, crowned them with victory garlands, patted them and proclaimed that the shoulders playing in the death-field of fierce cruelty were our victorious shoulders, and made poems of thought and counsel surge and swell with emotion like the Ganga, like the Kaveri!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 31 / printed 30 -->

Those who turned into viruttam the heroic cry of a mother who showed her belly and said, “I do not know where my son has gone; here is the cave in which that tiger lived!”—those who made into a song of the heart the rising of an old woman who shouted in fury: My son, who drank my milk, bore the enemy's spear on his back...... let this sword drink the blood of the breast from which he drank milk—those who turned into lion-striding verse the patriotism of the mother who combed his hair, adorned him with flowers and sent her pallid boy to the battlefield saying, “Father died. Brothers died. My young son! Come here! Here is the spear! Let this land be drenched with your blood too”—yes, they are the tigresses listed above!

The Dravidam in which they lived...... sits upon their graves, throws away the Tamil wealth they gave, and memorises the Mariamman lullaby. Purananuru has left here and, look, entered Russia.

In the last struggle, Idogiyo Nijulina flew by aeroplane 600 times, broke the enemies' ribs, carried out swift attacks and returned—Idogiyo Nijulina. Not a man! A woman! Woman!! Here, a cradle! There, an aeroplane! Here, an arasa tree! There, a laboratory!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 32 / printed 31 -->

Up to February 1, 1945, 10,55,000* women received many kinds of titles of heroic distinction because they took part in struggles.

There, Purananuru! Here, the Putradi Mariamman temple sindhu!

In organisations such as collective farms, up to the year 1940, 54,200 women held the posts of president, treasurer and secretary. In Russia!

Knowledge, in the country; the kitchen; this, the enemies'—Nijulina included, 49 women, breaking the backbone and rendering service to the country, have received the award “Soviet Union Hero” (Hero of the Soviet union).—“Ah! Character exactly like Sita Devi” “Modesty like Arundhati” “Beauty like Ahalya” “Chastity like Draupadi”—these titles here, in dark Dravidam!

Purananuru has been buried here. There......in Russia it shines.
