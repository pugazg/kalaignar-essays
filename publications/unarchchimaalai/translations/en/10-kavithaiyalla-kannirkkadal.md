# Not a Poem — an Ocean of Tears!

[The drops of tears shed while thinking  
of Selvam.]

<!-- Tamil source: scan 48 / printed numeral not visible -->

In a dark room, with both eyes shut,  
those who say the people of the world keep rolling—  
let ignorance sink! To make that understood  
Periyar's drum-sound thundered.  
Along that path came he...beloved...comrade,  
dear Selvam......warrior of the movement of reason.  
In the sun's good light,  
darkness withdrew, saying its time was done;  
there...  
the morning sun that rose in the east,  
if it suddenly fell...into the sea,  
would not the world be shaken? Would it not wither and waste away!  
Just as this land moved forward to cast off darkness and gain light,  
delusion-shrouded death-madness  
hid the young ray!

* * *

O one of the clan of the long-shouldered hill-lifter!  
With a cruel killing sword...the dwarf-jackal  
Aryanism,  
to uproot it, O lion-bull who emerged from the cave!  
O mountain-shouldered one! O ruby hill!  
`ப்ழச்சளை.` Your speech! A frost-blast in your gaze!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 49 / printed 48 -->

Parppaniyam, if it sees you,  
will reel like a coward at the sight of an army.  
You have gone...will your fame go with you?  
You set the Aryan forest ablaze! As it  
crackles and bursts, you hide away without watching—

O Panneer Selvam! Where is your milk-dripping  
face? Where is the Pandiyan moustache!  
Have the ruinous waves of the raging sea, swollen with hunger,  
ceased after devouring you! You left.  
“Let it eat; let it eat and rejoice...let  
Tiruvitaththar think of me”—saying so, where did you hide?  
Though born in money and raised in money,  
how did you learn proletarian character?  
O lord! The tears we shed, weeping and weeping,  
will conquer a thousand Oman Seas!  
O flawless golden Dravidian land! All the people  
who drank Aryan liquor, awakening and rising—  
to see this...is an offering to your work.  
Though opposition all around rages like a cyclone,  
we have set out declaring victory is ours!  
We are no paupers..........  
we are descendants of the guardian of Tanjai!  
Even if the time comes to fall, singing your deeds  
we shall fall.
