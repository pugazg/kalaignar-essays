# 3, 57, 90.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 33 / printed numeral not visible -->

“What audacity is this, man?”

“How did this man write this, man?”

“That degenerate speaks whatever comes into his mouth!”

“When a crowd follows us like a flock of sheep, what is this—does wisdom sprout only in him?”

“It is dangerous to leave the man alive.”

“Looks as though we cannot raise our heads while he is around.”

“That viper is scooping up and flinging poison, isn't he!”

Thus word-arrows were hurled one after another at that warrior. The warrior did not waver. He was diamond-hearted, one who would not care even if his ribs were broken or he were cut down. Neither the cowardice of hiding what was in his heart and speaking otherwise, nor the `kabothi` disposition of wishing to live by washing feet, nor, for self-interest,

<!-- Tamil source: scan 34 / printed 33 -->

the `sodha` disposition of standing around cheats ever came near his heart.

“Hey, atheist!......May you perish.”

“Poison tree of communalism......see if we let you live!”

He took these abuses as blessings.

“We need communal representation.”

He made that voice a little louder. He said it again and again. He said it often. He had come to dedicate his life to that alone. Aryanism, astonished that doctrines which people from Buddha to Ramalingan had trembled to utter were now being spoken boldly—and spoken at a time when rule of the country lay at the feet of our people......made its own syrupy speech into a sacrificial altar and began sacrificing Dravidian society upon it.

Alas.........how many fell upon that sacrificial altar! Kamarasars, Muthurangars, Bhaktavatsalars, Gramaniyars, Nadimuthus, Thiagarasar.........

“Brothers......do not slip and fall into communalism. We are sons who lay in the womb of Mother Bharata.”

<!-- Tamil source: scan 35 / printed 34 -->

Aryanism spoke while fingering the sacred thread.........the Dravidian Vibhishanas applauded with faint smiles curling......the Congress Party grew.

Only when communities disappear will communalism disappear......until then, for us to live—to prosper, communal representation is needed......Aryanism spreads its net of unity and says, there is only one caste; it turns all of you into `othiya` trees, sits in lofty seats and speaks of pleasure. It is a pack of wolves. That warrior set forth this idea. The Vediyars called him an atheist. The Vibhishanas called him a communalist. The awakened people called him a `mathith thalaivar`. There is one piece of news we must give so that the Vibhishanas may awaken—and those already awakened may gain clarity.

“The government is insisting on introducing a common-dining system at Tirupati Oriental College. The Devasthanam Committee requests the government to reconsider this order and to allow the scholarship allowance now being given to students who do not consent to take part in common dining to continue. But I understand that the government is not prepared to reconsider this. In the Oriental

<!-- Tamil source: scan 36 / printed 35 -->

College there are 150 students. There are 90 Brahmin students. There are only 3 Harijan students. Both Brahmin and non-Brahmin students are of one mind in wishing to leave the college if the common-dining system is introduced and if the scholarship allowance is stopped for those who wish to eat separately. I request the government to think about this again clearly—with broad-mindedness—and I also request the Devasthanam Committee to continue giving the scholarship allowance to students who refuse to accept the common-dining system.”

This statement by Sri Duraisami Iyengar, the Brahmanottama-clan lord who propagandises that the Dravidian crowd is spreading communalism in the country, appears in the Mail of 29-5-48.

There are one hundred and fifty college students. Of the Parppanar clan, who live as three in a hundred, there are ninety in that college! The Dravidian students, who are ninety-seven in a hundred, number fifty-seven there. Three are from the tribal class, left ruined as an uneducated community. By percentage: 60 in 100 are Parppanar; 38 in 100 are (non-Parppanar) Dravidians; 2 in 100 are

<!-- Tamil source: scan 37 / printed 36 -->

Adi-Dravidians. In this fine state of affairs, the lord of the Aryan clan wants to create a gurukulam there too. The crowd that sang the hymn that everyone is a great-penance-born son of Mother Bharata Devi now sings of division. It also brings along a few Dravidians devoid of self-respect as nodding dolls for it.

We......pitifully...with burning anguish......with tears streaming from our eyes, in the posture of begging...without letting go of the blood-tie...plead and implore those 57!.....O fifty-seven!...weigh and examine the act of the fanatical crowd that mocks communal representation...and uses Kilpauk Hospital for ridicule. You are 97 percent in the population. They are three percent. In the Oriental College you are 57; they are 90. Three are descendants of the lineage that toiled and toiled until transformed—turning blood into sweat—so that you and they may sit, move about and enjoy pleasure. The brain of Aryanism lays the foundation for pushing those three into a corner. It binds you too to itself and speaks, seeking strength.

Yes......just as it took Anjaneyas and Sugrivas as allies and sang the melody of victory—Sir

<!-- Tamil source: scan 38 / printed 37 -->

Ramasamis, Shanmugams having been lost, it protected its own community; likewise Duraisami Iyengar needs the support of your 57. He regards those three as three drops of poison; he regards your 57 as 57 `pira Prahaladas`, as 57 curry leaves.

You become sixty. Let Aryanism's ninety stand at that end. At least from now on, do not let Dravidian racial feeling fly away in the wind and burden yourselves with a blame that cannot be undone. This is what we must tell the government too.

Even if the ninety sacred threads—and why, even the fifty-seven of you—must leave the college, blind despite having eyes—savage in disposition, joining them in leaving, for those three—for those foundation stones of the new society...we turn into an “inner voice” the demand that you take up the courage to run the Oriyantal College. O great ones who call us communalists! O those who say the people of the Dravidian movement are narrow-minded! O those who stand against communal representation! Fix those numbers in your minds again! 3, 57, 90.
