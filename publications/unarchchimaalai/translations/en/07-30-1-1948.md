# 30-1-1948

<!-- Tamil source: scan 39 / printed numeral not visible -->

A blood-stained day in the history of the Indian subcontinent. The blood-tears the people shed ran like a waterfall, mingled in the Arabian Sea...in the Bay of Bengal, “flowing away......Has Gandhi died?” Before that question, the great sigh that rose shook the Himalayas—such a `soga poogam`.

The noble Gandhiyar was shot! Three times. Shot! Alas! Shot! He had come only for prayer, they say......not when, bearing spear and sword, he sternly asked where the foreign army was!......but when, in words overflowing with love, he asked whether the people had come for prayer......he was shot!

Not when he charged the enemy with eyes spitting fire...like a wild elephant that had hurled a dagger—but when, with eyes overflowing compassion, he rejoiced to see the people who had come to hear his teaching......he was shot! Shot....shot......`sukkunooraakkam`

<!-- Tamil source: scan 40 / printed 39 -->

On the 30th—Mohan Das Gandhi of the enchanting smile!......! On the 31st......Ayyo! Ayyo!...broken and burnt bone fragments!...... Three bullets fired by a fool ended the life of a martyr who had tried to drive away brutishness and ignorant religious frenzy.

“O bullet!...Are you too an accomplice to the treacherous deed done by that wicked man?...How could you...tear apart the heart of revered Gandhi, ripening with love?”...this continent weeps. Why...the whole world itself weeps.

The man who sucked away Gandhiyar's life was a `Parppanan`, yes...a lineage that brought empires down! A tradition that toppled kings of kings into the dust!...A drop of poison spat by that race plundered Gandhiyar's life. A treacherous sword hurled by that `Vaidheegam` cut down the life of the noble one of Wardha. The conspiracy wrought by that religious frenzy......gave a death sentence to a great warrior.......

India sheds tears.........the Hindu Mahasabha is said to have distributed sweets. We are not surprised! For we too live in the Dravidam where the Aryan crowd that distributed sugar when Sir Selvam's life was shattered in the Oman Sea lives! We have differences of opinion with Gandhiyar's principles. But we are not stone-hearted!...We are those who praised Gandhiyar's sacrifice and his noble labour

<!-- Tamil source: scan 41 / printed 40 -->

....and his zeal!....At the same time, we are those who never failed to say that Gandhiyar's service benefits many office-hunters and Baniya......Parppanars too!....

Gandhiyar!......You gave a great part of your labour as payment...and bought a measure of freedom for this continent!...Before freedom...could become true freedom...before the hunt for office could cease, you wrote that `thegiduthatham` had mixed into nationalism...and before that advice could set the rulers on the right path, you were destroyed!....

In the sixth month after ‘freedom’ was obtained—is this untimely death?—and that too for you?—Did you foster religious frenzy?—Did you disturb peace? Were you the cause of anarchy?—No!—Then why this murder! Religious frenzy—office frenzy—`Parppaniya` frenzy...is this the answer?—In independent India—to be swallowed by a mountain python of the very society for which you laboured—is shame, disgrace...an indelible stigma upon this subcontinent!.....Day 30-1-1948...yes...that squandered history—
