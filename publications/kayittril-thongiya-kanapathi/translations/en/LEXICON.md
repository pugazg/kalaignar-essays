# Living English Translation Lexicon — கயிற்றில் தொங்கிய கணபதி

This is the working consistency ledger for the English layer. It does not replace reading each Tamil sentence in context.

Permanent policy: [`../../../../ESSAY_TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md`](../../../../ESSAY_TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md)

## Rules

1. Preserve Kalaignar's source label, rhetorical function and emotional force.
2. Do not silently identify unexplained source-specific forms from memory or outside knowledge.
3. Contextual variants are allowed when the Tamil sense genuinely changes; record them here.
4. Political, social, religious and class-polemical language must not be politely neutralised.
5. If a Tamil source anomaly matters to meaning or quotation scope, document it rather than repairing it in English.
6. **Translate the language; do not neutralise the voice.**

## T2 watch-items

T2 should recheck, without reopening the frozen Tamil layer:

1. the working English title;
2. the irregular source quote beginning `மிகவும் உயர்ந்த நிலையில்...` and its T1 contextual rendering;
3. the source-bearing `மங்கு` → `mangu vessels` choice;
4. the colloquial quoted taunt `அடிப்பியோ? உங்க அப்பன் மவனே! சிங்கண்டா`;
5. `நன்றுக அழு!` → `Cry well!`;
6. the terminal slash in `அளிக்குமாக /`;
7. quotation-scope punctuation around the paragraph beginning `சுதந்திர பூமியில்...`.
