# The Killing Field!

---

<!-- Tamil source: scan 5 / printed numeral not visible -->

War—having turned lakhs of warriors into corpses; having drunk the lives of tender infants and young women as they trembled; having made crores of mothers cry out in anguish and then smiled a little smile at their wailing; having multiplied widows and laughed till its ribs hurt at their agony........having scratched at Mussolini's iron heart and tasted it, ended Hitler's life, handed Goering a cup of poison and feasted on his flesh, scraped Hiroshima with its crazed tongues and savoured it, and played in the mire of blood by planting bones there........war........unable to remain much longer behind the curtain called peace, now sings its dreadful song: ‘Here I come, here I come.’

War again? The world recoils. Another war before the wounds made by this war have even dried? Another war before the wound in the skull has healed? The war drum before ruined life has been repaired?

<!-- Tamil source: scan 6 / printed 6 -->

‘Yes, war,’ says Truman! Indifference drips from his speech. He belongs to a capitalist tradition that feels no anguish at seeing people's lives destroyed! The leader of a nation swollen with the pride that, at a mere ‘hmm’, it possesses an atom bomb capable of shaking the whole world!

Russia thunders: ‘We too have a bomb to answer the atom bomb!’

Nations intent on filling their arsenals.............nations laying plans for war camps.........nations that have not decided whether, in the coming chapter of war, they will be in a state of neutrality?........or a state of trembling?.............This is today's world!

When we read reports that American tanks are going to Russia... and of urgent action to stop them........when we are told that America is throwing itself with still greater intensity into atom-bomb production, calculating that it has raw materials enough to produce from thirty to five hundred atom bombs; when the Russian representative Gromyko condemns America and Britain for obstructing international control over atomic energy and also remarks that, watching America's course, peace cannot be established in the world; when we look closely

<!-- Tamil source: scan 7 / printed 7 -->

and when we hear that trench positions are being built all along the Soviet—U.S. boundary in the Soviet zone of Korea; when the New York paper ‘United States News’ reports that Britain has handed Malta over to America so that a great American naval base and air base may be built there; when U.S. President Truman, standing on the shoulders of labourers, sings a hymn to freedom—‘A life of slavery is far worse than war’; when the Danish Government orders that the existing air-defence bases must not be destroyed but must be kept in good condition, and even cancels military leave, so that the conch-blast of this danger warning falls upon our ears—we are compelled to believe that war is fast approaching.

On this war front, America and Britain will stand shoulder to shoulder and gird themselves to wipe Russia out.

Before the blood dries on the spear hurled at Hitler, Russia may have to take it into its own hand.

Britain—America—Russia, the three nations that stood together then to destroy Nazism, now stand apart, twirling their moustaches to build a tomb for peace.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 8 / printed 8 -->

The crores of devotees of Jesus, who preached the abolition of violence, want to stage swimming games in a sea of blood!

‘Beheading is a pastime for the king; for all of us it is the agony of life’...............At that level, sitting safely under protection...............the leaders deliver heroic speeches: ‘We shall invade! We shall destroy the world!’

Religion of righteousness—the guide of conciliatory virtue—the international organisation. With all this, still war!

A condition in which shame and anguish are mingled!

If this crisis is to be nipped in the bud, Stalin, Truman and Attlee must meet. Or Truman must be removed from the presidential chair. George Marshall must be driven out of the American administration—many warn with proposals such as these.

The blood-crazed leader will not lend an ear to these proposals!

War! War! War is going to come!

The volcano is rumbling! In just a little while, molten fire will be poured out, ladle after ladle!

**What then is India's position?**

<!-- Tamil source: scan 9 / printed 9 -->

‘We shall not hesitate to enter into a defence pact with Pakistan,’ says Pandit Nehru. That alone will not send us into rapture.

Looking at the course Hindustan's rule is taking, we are forced to think that, embracing America and Britain........though lacking strength, it may come to oppose Russia with mocking words!

Hindustan is a little child, by their own dictionary! It has not even reached crawling age! And a very weak infant at that!

We warn: do not commit infanticide—with the concern that the rulers' plan will affect Dravidian land too!
