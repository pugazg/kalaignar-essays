## Proper-name / source-label policy

- Follow the exact referential choice made in the Tamil sentence.
- Do not silently replace `ஆச்சாரியார்` or another source label with a modern full name inside the body.
- Conventional English spellings may be used for well-established international names, but source distinctions must not be erased.
- Article 1 uses **Truman**, **Gromyko**, **Stalin**, **Attlee**, **George Marshall**, **Pandit Nehru**, **Pakistan**, **Hindustan**, **Mussolini**, **Hitler**, **Goering** and **Hiroshima**.
- Periodical/publication names should retain their source identity. Article 1 retains the source-given **United States News**.
