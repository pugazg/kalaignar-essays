## Inherited authority through Article 36

All terminology and source-witness decisions established through Articles **1–36** remain active and are preserved in:

- [`LEXICON_THROUGH_16.md`](LEXICON_THROUGH_16.md) — historical terminology through Article 16;
- [`LEXICON_THROUGH_36.md`](LEXICON_THROUGH_36.md) — exact living lexicon state through Article 36, including Articles 17–36 additions.

These files and this continuation together form the active publication lexicon.

Most importantly, the permanent user-established rule remains:

- exact `உடன்பிறப்பே` → **`Udanpirappē`**;
- direct salutation → **`Udanpirappē,`**;
- never flatten it into `brother`, `sister`, `sibling`, `Dear sibling`, or `comrade`;
- source-bearing plural movement forms such as `Udanpirappugal` and `Kazhaga Udanpirappugalē` remain valid where the Tamil source uses them.

## Source-witness / non-regression decisions — Articles 37–50

- Article 37 translates all Naladiyar, Civaka Cintamani and Silappathikaram passages from frozen Tamil only. Verse lineation corrections were made in English before T5; Tamil remained unchanged.
- Article 38 preserves the spoken-`thanks` versus enacted-gratitude distinction and the final stone-inscription/sea-cataclysm image.
- Article 39 preserves Tolkappiyam’s one-to-six-sense taxonomy as the source presents it and retains common-noun `rationalism` distinct from the Article 27 journal `Pagutharivu`.
- Article 40 preserves the Periyar/feminism and 1989 property-right source claims without external reconciliation; `கைம்பெண்` was corrected in English to `widow` before T5.
- Article 41 contents `வலி அறிவிக்கும் வாயில்லா மொழி !` and heading `வலி அறிவிக்கும் வாயில்லா மொழி!` remain independent witnesses; English follows the heading.
- Article 42 preserves scientific/nuclear/disaster figures strictly as frozen-source claims. Source-bearing scientist-name forms are not silently modernised.
- Article 43 treats heaven/hell as Kalaignar’s reputation metaphor and does not convert the passage into translator theology.
- Article 44 deliberately keeps frozen-source `Kannan` in the Drona/Parashurama narrative; no outside Mahabharata correction is imported.
- Article 45 preserves both the source-visible title `இறையனார் குறளும்...` and source-visible chapter form `வலியுறுத்தல்`, even where external conventional forms might differ.
- Article 46 does not assign a numeric value to frozen irregular `ஓர் என்பது ஆண்டுகளுக்குள்`; no silent source correction was made. Bharathidasan quotation retains three-line display.
- Article 47 preserves `Udanpirappugal`, source `eighty years`, school-age `thalagaani urai`, Kannadasan quotations and finial→lighthouse metaphor.
- Article 48 contents title ends `!...` while heading ends `!`; English follows the heading. All lexical meanings come from the dictionary discussion already present in frozen Tamil, not new dictionary research.
- Article 49 crosses Part 004→005. All Chidambara Anthathi quotations remain source-derived; `Sengattu Nangai` was restored after T2 caught the omitted referent, and `கற்பழிக்க` remains `rape` rather than being softened.
- Article 50’s long Periyar and Anna quotations are translated only from frozen Tamil. A missed scan-222 sentence on speaking/writing as windows for a gentle breeze was restored before T5.

## Publication-level principles — still binding for E6/E7

- Translate the frozen Tamil authority; do not neutralise Kalaignar’s voice.
- Preserve questions, commands, repetition, satire, accusation, abrupt contrast, unusual source forms and concrete imagery.
- Preserve ordered source-scan comments in every English article.
- Translate embedded quotations from frozen Tamil only; do not import published English wording unless explicitly authorised.
- Keep names and epithets in the referential form chosen by the source; do not silently identify or modernise from outside knowledge.
- Keep independent contents-page and article-heading witnesses separate.
- Historical, literary, medical, political, scientific and news claims are translated as source claims; review is fidelity work, not external fact reconciliation.
- Reopen frozen Tamil only for a genuine defect demonstrated by controlling scan pixels. Article 16 scan 76 remains the standing precedent.
