## Publication-level principles established through Article 1 T4/T5

- Translate embedded quotations from the frozen Tamil wording; do not import published English versions unless explicitly authorised.
- Preserve commands, rhetorical questions, repeated punctuation, repetition, accusation, irony and abrupt contrast.
- Keep ordered Tamil source-page comments in every English article.
- Names/epithets follow the referential form chosen in the Tamil sentence; do not silently modernise or expand them from outside knowledge.
- Remove unsupported added specificity even when the English reads smoothly; fidelity takes priority over explanatory convenience.
- Preserve repeated metaphors and deliberately forceful imagery even when a smoother English paraphrase would sound more conventional.
- Prefer natural English for a culturally marked image when its rhetorical action survives intact; use transliteration when natural English would erase or falsely identify the source-bearing term.
- T4-confirmed choices remain contextual. Later articles may require documented variants where the Tamil function genuinely changes.
