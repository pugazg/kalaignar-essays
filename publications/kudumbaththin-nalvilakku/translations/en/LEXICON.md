## Final consistency decisions

- **family welfare programme** and **family planning** are a deliberate source distinction, not terminology drift.
- **Tamil Nadu Government / Delhi Government / Central Government** are deliberate source-label distinctions.
- **Lord Shiva / Shiva / Parama Sivan** are deliberate source-label distinctions reflecting the Tamil.
- Literal and metaphorical **invasion** remain deliberately repeated.
- **Kayilaiyangiri** remains source-bearing and must not be conventionalized from outside knowledge.
- E6 required no terminology correction; E7 confirmed the lexicon without rewriting it.
