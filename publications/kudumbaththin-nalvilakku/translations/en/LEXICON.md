## Non-regression

- Frozen Tamil controls every English choice.
- User-reviewed old-glyph forms are not editorial errors to be modernised during translation.
- Prefer source-bearing transliteration or restrained translation when a culturally marked label cannot be safely expanded from the article itself.
- External research is not part of ordinary translation unless explicitly requested.
- Add terms as they arise during T1; record contextual exceptions rather than hiding them.
