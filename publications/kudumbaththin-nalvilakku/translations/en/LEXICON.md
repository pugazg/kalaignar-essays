## T1 non-regression

- Frozen Tamil controls every English choice.
- User-reviewed old-glyph forms are not editorial errors to be modernised during translation.
- The draft deliberately distinguishes `குடும்பநலத் திட்டம்` → **family welfare programme** from `குடும்பக் கட்டுப்பாடு` → **family planning**.
- The repeated literal/metaphorical `படை எடுப்பு` chain remains **invasion** to preserve Kalaignar's analogy.
- Source-bearing names such as **Pillaiyar**, **Kuchelan**, **Krishna Paramatma** and **Kailaiyangiri** are not silently replaced with alternate identifications.
- `ஆலாய்ப் பறந்தார்கள்` and the contextual force of `மூர்த்தன்யமாக` are flagged for explicit T2/T3 recheck.
- External research is not part of ordinary translation unless explicitly requested.
