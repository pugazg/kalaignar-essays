# The Rooster Crows!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 53 / printed 51 -->

As resolved by the General Council of the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam when it met in Madurai, about one hundred and fifty people signed the DMK pledge agreement. The committees of the respective districts met and decided to support roughly seventy of them in the election.

Of those seventy, twenty-three candidates in South Arcot district have received our support.

There are twenty-three seats in South Arcot district. In all twenty-three places, candidates supported by our Kazhagam are contesting.

Heroes who raise the voice of Dravida Nadu! Mongooses confronting the cobras that oppose separation!

Lamps of reason that expose to the country the selfish demons hiding in dark caves and roaring that separation must not take place!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 54 / printed 52 -->

In South Arcot district, the Workers' Party is the party laying the foundation for the Congress's tomb. Comrade S. S. Ramasami, a servant of public welfare, a close friend of the workers, and commander of the Workers' Party, together with the comrades who labour shoulder to shoulder with him, are engaged in the sacred task of ending cruelty. There is no place in South Arcot where these heroes have not moved! Not only at election time: the friends of the Workers' Party are diamonds who live thinking of South Arcot as their home and of the people of South Arcot as their family.

Into such a South Arcot, where warriors of ideals march and where the descendants of the Three Crowned Kings stand guard over that grove of Muttamil, cattle dare to enter. The most cruel news of all is that northern Koyanga has come to oppose the lineage of Valluvar!

The imperishable treasure of Dravida is the Tirukkural; the man who bears that Kural is Tirukkural Munisami, who supports Dravidian separation!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 55 / printed 53 -->

Koyanga's wealth is wealth extracted from Dravida!

These—two kinds of wealth collide!

Vibhishanas and Sugrivas are on Koyanga's side! In the Congress army!

The heroes, all those who possess the feeling of self-respect, are on our side!

The Congress must hurry, hurry to its grave. The people of South Arcot have sounded the conch to undertake that sacred task.

South Arcot! Ever-burning jewel-lamp of Dravida! Honeyed song! Sweet fruit of Dravida! The time has come to show your ability—make ready! Men of action of South Arcot with lion-hearts!

Let the decayed Congress perish in your laughter! Money will play! The Congress battalion will work! Fear not, tire not!

The voice of Dravida Nadu is going to sound in the Assembly, in Parliament! Find strength for it! Raise your shoulders, come and serve! The DMK—

<!-- Tamil source: scan 56 / printed 54 -->

—has supported more than seventy people! Twenty-three are from South Arcot!

In a loud voice, with the sound of feeling, let the sweet-Tamil *parani* of Dravida be sung—make the Congress turn its back!

South Arcot, let the lesson you give teach the Congress some sense!

Let the flag of victory you send flying place upon the bier the rule that took our lives to make us tremble, starved us, and bred famine!

“Dravida Nadu for the Dravidians alone!” South Arcot! Say it aloud! Say it with fervour!

Workers' Party, send your flag flying high!

The cow box is the box whose *mangalyam* has snapped! The box that turned human life to dust! The box that bred cruelty! The box that conducted crooked rule!

“The rooster box is the box of heroes. To announce that the darkness of suffering is lifting—to show that the dawn-star of liberation is rising, to tell us that the red sun is coming up—the rooster crows! Hail the rooster! Give it your votes! Victory to South Arcot! Down with the Congress!
