# A Special Warning to the Public


<!-- Tamil source: scan 24 / printed 22 -->

After we announced at the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam State Conference our decision on whom to support in the election, the Communist paper *Janasakthi* wrote that the resolution was deeply disappointing and would help Kamarajars and Samiyappas win.

Why the Communists—who in fact shake hands with those officially working for Samiyappa's victory—should heap this baseless charge upon us, we do not understand!

If we refuse to live by selling our shame and joining those who called our movement "cast-offs," to say that we are therefore helping our enemies to win is thoroughly false propaganda.

At the very moment we extended the hand of friendship, they brushed it aside, insulted our fundamental issue, and called us only with the words, "Come just for the election." And now they say, "Not to drink the honey of the election"—

<!-- Tamil source: scan 25 / printed 23 -->

—and point out that they did not invite us in order to form a Communist government. These remain riddles we cannot understand.

Samiyappa took pains in Thanjavur district to work for the destruction of the Communists. After making platforms thunder with speeches that it would be a great cruelty to let him win, after painting word-pictures to that effect in their papers, what name are we to give to the act of standing in fellowship with those who support him? We do not know.

A feast in the capitalist's house on one side; a sermon on wiping away the worker's suffering on the other! What a contradiction to the courage—and the policy—of the Communists! Was it for such a grotesque United Front that we invited them?

If we had been working together, would we oppose K. T. Rasu in Erode? Would we try to defeat Mohan Kumar Mangalam in Salem?

At a meeting in Kanchipuram, comrade Jeevanandam said: "Periyar asked us to give way to Samiyappa—

<!-- Tamil source: scan 26 / printed 24 -->

—give way to that murderous sinner?"

According to Jeevanandam's own dictionary, the Communist mind can cultivate relations with the mentality that asked them to yield to a murderous sinner! But we invited them to an orderly—firm—unmutilated United Front, and the Communists could not agree to it! Not only did they refuse agreement; they began to reject the very question that is life itself to us.

They can maintain relations with those who say that *Janasakthi* is not a Communist paper! But they are afraid even to approach us—who respect *Janasakthi*, try to answer its doubts, and desire friendship!

Policy has reached such a state that they can keep friendship with those who refused to recognise the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam as an organisation and failed to understand democracy, yet they tremble at friendship with a great force functioning democratically!
