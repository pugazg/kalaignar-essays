# Youngsters

<!-- Tamil source: scan 37 / printed 35 -->

G. P. Somasundaram is about sixty years old! K. K. Neelamegam is about fifty! Pollachi Natarasu is about sixty! Pethampalayam Palanisami is nearly forty! Nedunchezhiyan is about thirty! Salem Chithaiyan is about forty! E. V. K. Sampath is about twenty-seven. C. P. Chitrarasu is about forty! N. V. Natarasan is about forty! Parthasarathi is definitely forty-two! Appaduraiyar is about fifty! Manimozhiyar is probably fifty-four! S. V. Lingam is an old man indeed! Elderly Ramamirthammal is thirty years old! (Twice over.) Arangannal is about twenty-two! Eelathu Adigal is about fifty! I am exactly twenty-nine!

The ages given in the list above were not calculated from horoscope notes, nor from the year, month and date—

<!-- Tamil source: scan 38 / printed 36 -->

—they were written down from looking at them, from estimates, from what I had previously asked some of them, and from what I could remember to some extent!

Are you asking, "What's this, man? Why have you suddenly plunged into age calculations?" Except for one or two of those mentioned, all of them are married comrades; so no one should mistake this for a marriage application. If it were that sort of matrimonial application, wouldn't I have reduced the ages before stating them!

You laugh when I say that all those mentioned above are youngsters, don't you? But they certainly are young men!

Periyar Ramasamy used to say often in earlier days: "Even if I have grown old, my heart is still a young man's heart!" Likewise, all the old men and youths in the DMK are people with young hearts. And not only that: the rules of the DMK itself lay down that only those above eighteen years of age—

<!-- Tamil source: scan 39 / printed 37 -->

—can be admitted as members.

Yet, for some reason I do not know, poor G. P. Somasundaram and people like Pollachi Natarasu are called youngsters. Under government law, those above eighteen are adults who are no longer "minors"! Surely no one would call them youngsters without even knowing that. "Youngsters" means people who possess young hearts even in old age!

And when I say "one with a young heart," there is our old man S. V. Lingam! He seems to be planning a marriage! No, no.... I am only joking! Lingam Anna, wherever you may be now, don't go making any such arrangements! Where are you going to make arrangements anyway! You've become a swami, haven't you! Do you have property! Comfort! An heir! A clod of earth!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 40 / printed 38 -->

Older comrades? Some of you may have been left out. But do not imagine that all of you are youngsters! Grown-ups with young hearts! A young heart is a heart of ideals! Not a heart of love! Oh-ho-ho! I forgot one thing! Even our commander Anna is over forty years old!
