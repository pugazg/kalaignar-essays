# Think and Understand! Do Not Grow Angry!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 71 / printed 69 -->

In Tiruppattur, Comrade Raghavan, who received the support of the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam and signed the agreement document, has won; in Madurai, Communist Comrade Ramamurthi has won; in the Udayagiri constituency Finance Minister Gopalar has been defeated; in the Amalapuram constituency Kala Venkata Rao has been defeated—these are regarded as tidings that pour honey into Dravida.

Gopalar—a minister for Finance! Kala too—a minister for Health! Both had been praised as pillars of the Congress mansion. Those pillars have been smashed to dust.

Once, in the legislature, when he was asked, “You are increasing the burden of taxes like this; will the yellow box get votes in the election?” Gopalar replied, “If the yellow box will not get votes, we can simply change the box.”

<!-- Tamil source: scan 72 / printed 70 -->

Even after changing the yellow box into the cow box, Gopalar and Kala have both had to fall into the lap of defeat!

At Tiruppattur, our friend Raghavan, whom we supported, has defeated the Congress by polling six thousand more votes. Our praise to him!

At Madurai, our Communist friend Ramamurthi has smashed the Congress and sounded the drum of victory. From within prison itself, he has turned the influence of the party holding the sceptre into worthless coin! He deserves our praise and joy!

As we praise Ramamurthi's victory, so too in some places we expect the victories of our Communist friends. We welcome them. We wish them victory. In places where candidates who have not signed the DMK agreement document are contesting, and in places where those who have signed are not accepted, to whom else could the votes of our Kazhagam members go if not to our Communist friends!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 73 / printed 71 -->

Anna's statement has pointed this out too. Even if the DMK does not officially support the Communists in such places, how DMK members will act when casting their votes—and how they have acted—the consciences of Comrades Ramamurthi, Anantha Nambiar, Manali Kandasami, Muthiah, Mohan and others certainly cannot hide! Those who have regard for honesty and justice—sensible people who harbour no poisonous intent—can understand that truth.

If we have had to oppose Jeevanandam in Madras, it is only because Comrade Albert Jesudasan has given an assurance that he will raise in the legislature the DMK's fundamental ideal, Dravida Nadu, and has signed our agreement! As the Communist friends say, Albert Jesudasan is not an unknown newcomer! He is one who was in the struggles of the Dravidian movement. He was one who took part in the anti-Hindi struggle.

At a time when the Communists watched the anti-Hindi struggle in silence, no one can deny that Albert was on the field of righteous struggle.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 74 / printed 72 -->

“They are opposing even Jeevanandam!” cry some, stirring up trouble, who have made throwing dignity away their very duty! I do not know why those same people forget that they themselves are opposing Mohan Kumaramangalam and K. D. Raju.

Do they think Mohan Kumaramangalam is inferior to Jeeva? Or is it some firm prophetic omen that Mohan will get fewer votes than Comrade Balu of Salem—that Balu alone can win?

Mohan Kumaramangalam belongs in Jeeva's own rank.

Those comrades who oppose such a Mohan and support Comrade Balu make much of our opposition to Jeeva and set about poisonous work.

Whether they understand this or not—or whether, at election time, they pretend not to understand because they feel they should not show that they understand—the Communist friends too hurl mocking words at us.

“Maalai Mani devotees”
“Anna has come to pour forth grace”

<!-- Tamil source: scan 75 / printed 73 -->

“They are giving up their lives”
“Anna has come to grant refuge as the saviour of orphans”
“Blank cheque”

Through Janasakthi they hurl taunts like these at our Kazhagam!

In a few constituencies such as North Madras, where we have been placed in a position of opposing Comrade Jeevanandam, our comrades may perhaps ignore these taunts and continue working.

But in places where Nambiar, Kalyanasundaram, Muthiah, Manali and others are standing, the Communist friends and the Janasakthi paper must think about how much pain will be felt in the hearts of our Kazhagam comrades who want their victory and are ready to cast their votes for them. However much a spear may be thrust into a raw wound, the Communist friends must understand that the votes of Kazhagam members will not go to the Congress; they will understand.

The Communist friends, who are prepared for Periyar to support Sharma—whom the Communists describe as a slave of imperialism—and are prepared to work together with Periyar, somehow heap baseless accusations upon us. I do not understand why.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 76 / printed 74 -->

Albert Jesudasan said he would raise the Dravida Nadu question in the legislature. Jeeva said that was not possible. We support Albert. Where no such situation exists, not only do we not oppose the Communists; even if we do not extend formal Kazhagam support, the feeling has spread that we shall give our votes to the Communists themselves. We are not people with the perverse course of saying that we support the Communists while opposing Mohan, K. D. Raju and many other Communist friends.

Along with asking the Communists to understand this, we ask those who stir up trouble—and the general public thrown into confusion by that trouble—to think carefully and understand, and to know the truth. Along with that, we offer our sympathy to the defeated Congress ministers and our joy to the victorious heroes.
