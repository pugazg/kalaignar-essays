# Boom! Boom! Boom!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 77 / printed 75 -->

Will good times dawn for Ayya?
Boom, boom, boom!
Will hunger go away?....
Boom, boom, boom!
Will starvation disappear?
Boom, boom, boom!
Will the trade in children stop?
Boom, boom, boom!
Will suicides subside?
Boom, boom, boom!
Will honey and milk flow?
Boom, boom, boom!
And then, will Ayya cast his vote?
Boom, boom, boom!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 78 / printed 76 -->

To all of these, those Perumal cattle nod their heads. Do you know how beautifully the Perumal cattle are decorated?

On their foreheads is an Ashoka Chakra made of diamonds. Only to the eye are they diamonds; they are all the broken teeth of working people. (Knocked out in the lathi-charge durbar.)

Yellow has been smeared on their horns. It appears yellow, but it is the blood of this country's workers! It has dried and so appears yellow.

Around the cattle's necks, silver amulets glitter; are they amulets! No; no! They have strung together the bones of citizens of this free country who curled up and died of hunger and agony.

Across the cattle's backs are *pitambaras* and zari shawls draped by the Birlas of the North—with all this finery, the Congress Perumal cattle nod their heads to the boom, boom of the instrument.

“Then, will Ayya cast his vote?
Boom, boom, boom!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 79 / printed 77 -->

The cattle nod their heads: he will vote!

A little boy ran up to the Perumal cattle-handler.
Want oilcake?
Want tamarind seeds?
Want cottonseed?
Boom, boom, boom, boom! he began to shout.

The Perumal cattle-handler got angry. Unable to say anything in his rage, he sounded the instrument: boom, boom, boom.
When he stopped, the cattle began nodding their heads.
That nodding made it seem as though they were saying: want oilcake, want cottonseed!

Hey, cow that told us to eat oilcake!
Hey, cow that told us to eat cottonseed!
Here—oilcake! The boy came running with it. At once the Perumal cattle-handler pleaded, “Little master, bring your vote and put it in! This cow eats only votes.”

The little master's father kept smiling.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 80 / printed 78 -->

“Look, sir, look—some old cloth, anything; cast one vote,” he said, baring his teeth.
He remained silent.

Will Ayya vote?
Boom, boom, boom!
The cattle nodded.

The householder went inside.
The Perumal cattle-handler could not contain his joy.
“Ayya has gone inside—will he bring a vote and cast it?” Boom, boom, boom.
“Ayya will vote? Boom, boom, boom!”

Ayya, who had gone inside, did put something on!
What?
The bolt on the door!

The Perumal cattle-handler, stunned, said in a feeble voice:
“Ayya won't vote, then? Boom, boom, boom!”
The cattle kept nodding.
Boom! Boom!! Boom!!!
