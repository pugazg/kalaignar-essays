## Rhetorical / quotation policy for this publication

- preserve direct address, questions, commands, exclamations, mock praise, sarcasm and accusatory cadence;
- preserve source quotation scope; do not turn quoted political speech into indirect narration;
- Article 7 — preserve the quoted-title framing of `“அஹிம்சா விலாசம்”`;
- Article 11 — translate the Silappathikaram/Kovalan analogy from the frozen source without importing outside literary explanation;
- Article 13 — preserve the repeated `பூம்` call-and-response pattern and its deliberate comic timing;
- source-specific names, abbreviations and election-symbol language should remain source-bearing when an English equivalent would over-resolve the reference.
