# Heroes of Retreat!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 21 / printed 19 -->

The bravest of the brave took fright and fled. Ministers who rule the land ran on all fours. Men who command an army that shoots a man down at the twitch of an index finger ran. Men equipped with chariots, elephants, cavalry and infantry fled in panic. From whom?

Did they see the soldiers of an enemy country? Did they hear Britain's war-drum? Did they see the Americans marching? Did they hear that Russian troops were coming? No.... No........ They took to their heels at the sight of a clown;

(Forgive me........ "clown" is the medal of abuse Kamarasar bestowed upon Kalaivanar Krishnan.)

A conference at Srivilliputhur in which ministers were to participate!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 22 / printed 20 -->

Yes! Born upon this holy land, blessed by Mother Bharat, endowed by the grace of the inner soul with the power to achieve whatever they think of—(the power to achieve what they think of means ordering a trade in children and piling up mountains of corpses)—the great and mighty holy men seated upon ministerial thrones apparently judged that even looking upon the face of the sinner who had been to Russia would be a pollution, and so sprang away with their heels striking the backs of their heads.

The lords who wield the sceptre trembled at the clown's voice. Srivilliputhur is the constituency in which Kamarasar is contesting! In that place, the oppressed wished to hold a conference. Those who screamed themselves hoarse claiming to labour for the oppressed ran away after humiliating the heroes of the durbar. The man who had been to Russia—if he were to tell the oppressed of the joys that flourish in Russia...... if all those oppressed people were then to shout aloud at our famine ministers, "O rulers who came to govern! Listen, sir, to Russia's merriment!"—what would they do? It was out of that fear that they ran.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 23 / printed 21 -->

An army all around—the sceptre of office in hand.... revolvers—despite possessing all these instruments, the ruler of the land (Kumarasami Raja) has hidden himself in fear of the king of comedy!

Long live this fear! This "flight" may, in the language of war, count as a victorious retreat. Long live this "retreat"!
