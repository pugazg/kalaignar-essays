# Ministers Tremble in Fear

<!-- Tamil source: scan 17 / printed 15 -->

In the Srivilliputhur constituency—where the Chief Minister of Madras Province was standing for the Assembly and Virudhunagar's strange man, Kamarasar, for Parliament as Congress candidates—the Congressmen convened a conference. Ministers were scheduled to speak there; Minister Parameswaran, along with ministers such as Kumarasami Raja and Perumalsami Reddiar, had gone to the place.

But those who organised the conference, wishing to enjoy and enlarge their knowledge by listening to the bow-song *Naan Kanda Russia* ("The Russia I Saw") of Kalaivanar, the king of comedy, N. S. Krishnan, had invited him as well. Kalaivanar N. S. Krishnan arrived with his troupe and began his bow-song. We have received a report that, the moment the ministers learnt this, they slipped away without telling anyone and without taking part in the conference. If this report is true, there can be no act more shameful than this.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 18 / printed 16 -->

Kalaivanar N. S. Krishnan had gone to Russia and is now presenting beautifully, in the form of a bow-song, what he himself saw there. Through that bow-song our people are able to learn: how do the Russians run their country? What lofty condition has Russia reached today?

If Congress ministers take to their heels faster than the wind at the sight of this, what are we to call it? Why should these ministers run? In Russia the rains do not fall regularly. So, without depending upon the rain, they have discovered and kept ready the technique of producing the food they need! Therefore they do not fear famine. Even in the most severe wartime crisis there, no food crisis arose. Moreover, people's lives there have been made prosperous. The suffering of life is not to be found there even in trace amounts. As a result, crimes such as theft, gambling, fraud and murder are rare. Since people live with every facility and without want, there is no opposition to the government there. Nor is there burning resentment among the people. A life of peace, calm and happiness—

<!-- Tamil source: scan 19 / printed 17 -->

—flourishes among the people there: this is what Kalaivanar explains through his bow-song.

The ministers must have known very well that, if the people understood these truths, they would also understand the causes of the Congress regime's lack of honesty, incompetence, want of integrity, selfishness and other disorders; that the people would realise that the famine prevailing in the country, the spread of bribery and corruption, and the black market becoming ubiquitous were all evils produced by the selfishness of Congressmen; and that, if the people then challenged them with questions while they spoke, they would be left struggling and choking for want of a proper answer. That must be why these ministers ran away instead of taking part in the conference.

For Congress rulers, standing before a public forum, giving satisfactory answers to the people's questions, and setting their minds at rest is an impossible task. What else can they do but flee at the sight of honesty?

<!-- Tamil source: scan 20 / printed 18 -->

Winning an election honestly, with integrity, on the strength of policy—there is no such thing in the Congress dictionary. Since victory can be secured by some intrigue or other, by importing someone from the North and dragging him around here as a spectacle, by employing many other tricks, by using official power and taking the police as an ally, the Congress rulers have hardened their hearts into believing that there is no need to stand before a public forum at all; that they can gather black-marketeers, permit-holders, licence-holders and contractors somewhere, display those gatherings as public forums, and deceive those who speak of democracy. What else can such rulers do but run from the truth!

Alas! If Congressmen of this kind return to power, what will become of the country! Therefore, keeping all this in mind, let the people refuse their votes to Congressmen and, from among those standing against them, choose one—just one—and make that candidate victorious.........
