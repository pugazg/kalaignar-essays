# Election Kovalan!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 63 / printed 61 -->

“The Congress has rusted. Greedy for office as though it were laddu and puri, many unworthy fellows have entered it.”

“After the Congress came to power, many Congressmen became lazy and pleasure-loving. Because of this, the Congress organisation has been affected and weakened.”

Some may think the sentences above are words circling out from Kanchi. Certainly not.

They are not ideas hurled from Kanchi, nor from the Communist camp!

It is the Gandhian heir himself who has uttered these fire-spitting words!

The stage when people said Kripalani said it, Prakasam said it, Ranga said it, he said it, this man said it, has passed. That Nehru himself has said it has now become a familiar and pitiable piece of news.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 64 / printed 62 -->

Nehru, who came hunting for votes among the people of Bihar, has finished reciting this lament once.

Nehru has conducted many such concerts of lamentation. At election time this lament is sung in a rapid tempo.

He expects people to garland him with praise and say: “Look at Nehru! He alone is pure! See how he condemns Congressmen engaged in base acts!”

Nehru's rule has lasted for about five years!

All that time, he did not speak about honesty, did not come to preach justice and fairness, did not come to shed tears over the collapse of party discipline. If he suddenly pours out tears, beats his head in agitation, writhes and leaps about, what does that mean!

There are certain speeches that belong specifically to election time!

They are the vows babbled out during a lovers' quarrel in the bedchamber. There is no agreement document for them;

<!-- Tamil source: scan 65 / printed 63 -->

there are many Sundarangathars who cry, “My dear! My jewel! My wish-fulfilling tree! My golden statue!” shower love upon her, abuse a few other women to win her affection, make a few promises by word of mouth, and forget what they said when dawn comes!

There are many of that kind in elections too!

There are many charming Kovalans who beg forgiveness saying, “Forget what I did! Listen to what I say. It was wrong to seek Madhavi's bed,” and, from Kannagi who is bewitched by those words, receive the anklet and go away! The Kovalan of the *Silappathikaram*, after receiving forgiveness, destroyed himself and also destroyed his wife—because Kannagi gave him the anklet!

These election Kovalans too plan to obtain the anklet called the vote, take the Kannagi called the voter across forest and hill, get caught with the Vanjipattar called America, and ruin everyone's lives.

That is the ending we expect in the serial tale of sorrow being written by the Congressmen.

Kovalan, in the *Silappathikaram*, truly asked forgiveness. He tried to reform himself.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 66 / printed 64 -->

These Kovalans, however, want to end the *Silappathikaram* at Vanjipattar's house. Yes, it is about to end with enslavement to America. The final chapter of this wretched tale! They want to bind Dravida—to bind us—to the tragic ending they are themselves eager to seek.

Many speak in Nehru's voice.

“Forget what has been done! Let us do properly what we do from now on,” they say.

Only after leaving Madhavi behind did Kovalan ask forgiveness. That forgiveness was just, too!

But these people, without stopping their wrongs and while continuing them still; tightening their embrace toward American domination; refusing even at election time to release political prisoners and keeping them shut in prison; refusing to remove bans on the plays and books of the rationalist movement; delaying the cases they have brought against opposition parties and pouring hardships upon them; declaring, because rain has fallen, that the South can no longer be sent the food it needs—

<!-- Tamil source: scan 67 / printed 65 -->

—while sending out such cruel statements, they beg us to forget their wrongs. They shed tears. Among tears there is a kind called Neeli tears! The people are not ignorant of it!

Those who point out the wrongs—those who say, “Forget them,” and beg for the alms of support—belong to the class that asks us to trust a merciless man who speaks sweetly without removing the spear lodged in the heart!

We are not unaware of those who turn into a breeze at election time and into a storm after victory, nor of those who have already done so. The people are not unaware. That awareness cannot be erased by new sermons! The Travancore-Cochin election results echo precisely this.

When one counts the total votes received by the Congress and all the parties that opposed it, the opposition parties have received a large majority of the votes.

Not only that: the opposition parties have captured more seats than the Congress.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 68 / printed 66 -->

Because its opponents have become many, the Congress has beaten the drum of victory in a few places—but it sounds only like a rain-soaked drum.

Somehow, a similar situation has arisen in Tamil Nadu too. In every place, many are contesting against the Congress. It is a situation in which the Congress can win with fewer votes! Yet somehow this situation has come about! Since there is no electoral relationship between the Communists and the DMK, in some places we have been forced to oppose Communist candidates. Even though there is an electoral relationship between the Communists and the Dravidar Kazhagam, the Dravidar Kazhagam has found itself opposing prominent Communists such as Mohan Kumaramangalam and K. D. Raju.

As Anna's statement says, because we have made the Dravida Nadu question the basis, in some places we have had to oppose the Communists.

The Dravidar Kazhagam opposes Communists in places where there is a chance of victory.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 69 / printed 67 -->

Although both camps generally have the destruction of the Congress as their election programme, the DMK has added the Dravida Nadu question to it as well.

Some candidates supported by the DMK are also supported by the Dravidar Kazhagam. Anna's statement explains this. But the Dravidar Kazhagam supports them without an agreement. The DMK supports the same candidates after obtaining an agreement from them.

The new condition—the new gain—created by the DMK agreement has grown beyond merely destroying the Congress: it also means raising the voice of the Dravida Nadu question in the Legislative Assemblies and People's Assemblies.

Even if the Dravidar Kazhagam did not ask for this agreement—or failed to ask for it—we made the Dravida Nadu question, which is also the Dravidar Kazhagam's goal, the fundamental election question.

When those who receive our support thunder, “Dravida Nadu for the Dravidians alone!” in the Legislative Assemblies—in the People's Assemblies—the hearts of both the DMK and the Dravidar Kazhagam will swell.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 70 / printed 68 -->

It is to prevent that swelling of the heart that many in Tamil Nadu speak in Nehru's style.

Many who take care that the basic conditions for Dravida's separation must not arise say, “Forget what has been done! Look at what we are going to do,” and tempt the people.

To leave no room for the doubt that, because of circumstances, the votes of those opposing the Congress may be split, the Dravidian people must refrain from putting even a single vote into the cow box.

Only if a hundred votes fall into it can the Congress defeat two candidates who receive eighty and sixty.

Therefore we ask the Dravidian people not to be deceived by the Neeli tears shed by Nehru and those who speak in his voice; not to forget the Dravida Nadu question; to hold in their hearts that if Dravida separates, evils will leave! They will leave!—and to come forward to destroy the Congress.

There was clarity in the forgiveness sought by the Kovalan of the *Silappathikaram*. There was yearning.

There is no clarity in the forgiveness sought by the Election Kovalan! Deceit too lies hidden within it!
