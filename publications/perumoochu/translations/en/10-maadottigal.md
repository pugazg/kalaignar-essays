# Cattle-Drivers!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 57 / printed 55 -->

On another day, those who came to exploit Dravida came driving cattle through the passes of the Himalayas!

Even now, those who come to us driving cattle are labouring to turn Dravida into a deer caught in the northerner's net.

They are slaves of the North. The cattle they drive are trying to make us too slaves of the North for many more years.

It is not merely an attempt to stamp northern civilisation, northern language and northern culture upon Dravida; through these cattle-drivers, an intense effort is under way to turn Dravida into the North's permanent lackey.

They have acquired the nerve to think that the people can be dazzled by saying: it was the Congress that won liberation for us; it was the Congress that brought us freedom!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 58 / printed 56 -->

The fact that they won freedom does not mean that we must lose all the comforts of our lives!

Nor did freedom come solely through their ability and sacrifice.

We attained freedom; we too rejoiced. Even then, we did not fail to point out that the crisis that had overtaken the white man, the situation he faced after the World War—the condition in which he could no longer hold India together—were also important reasons for liberation.

Yes, it is a land of freedom! But is it not here that we have mountains of corpses! Markets for children! Suicides! Self-appointed durbars!

Is the tyranny in the northerner's hand what you call freedom!

Is a durbar of repression what you call freedom!

If Dravida had obtained self-rule, would corpses have fallen here!

If Dravida had exercised separate rule, would Dravidians have fled to Malaya and Ceylon!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 59 / printed 57 -->

Would not a free Dravida have brought back all the Dravidians suffering hardships in Ceylon and given them a sweet life!

In Malaya, Ganapathi had to hang from a rope.

For Sambasivam, merely escaping became a great thing.

And only a few days ago, Malaya's stage of death swallowed Loganathan and belched!

The government that rules us is a member of the Commonwealth.

It has no courage to speak of the cruelty in Malaya and do what is proper. Nor has it developed the feeling that it should leave the Commonwealth. Had Dravida been a separate country, would we have allowed Ganapathi and Loganathan to die? Why—would they even have been in a position where they had to go and live in Malaya?

They committed wrongs. They forgot to condemn the wrongs inflicted abroad! Yet they come driving only the cattle, asking for votes.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 60 / printed 58 -->

Is it not because we are in the grip of the North that Koyanga can stand as a candidate in the South?

Is it not because we are in the grip of the North that some of Birla's agents—scattering Birla's money—contest elections here in Dravida?

It is precisely because Dravida must be freed from such cruel imperial domination, precisely because Dravida must become a free land, precisely because true equality must prevail in Dravida, that we raise the cry: ‘Dravida Nadu for the Dravidians alone’

The cattle-drivers do not like this cry; they not only dislike it, they reject it.

When called “slaves of the North,” they leap in anger. What else are we to call those who say that the language, culture and questions of the North—suited to that land—must all be accepted unchanged in Dravida, if not slaves of the North?

Those who bring Koyanga and tell him to contest in Dravida—calling them slaves of the North—

<!-- Tamil source: scan 61 / printed 59 -->

—does not seem wrong to us!

“We have explained many times before the people that Dravida is a separate country. Its questions and institutions cannot be looked at only through one eye, from one angle, using the questions and institutions of another country.”

But some, in the manner of “the hare I caught has only three legs,” merely prophesied that it was impossible, impracticable, narrow-minded; merely tried to hide our heart's song by dragging in another country as an example; became slaves to doctrines and enemies of practice; and remained people who did not understand the inner meaning of our question.

Those who showed themselves openly as enemies opposed our question directly! Those who did not show themselves did so indirectly. It was to bring all these forms of opposition to an end that we decided to support in the election those who accept the Dravida Nadu question.

The victory of those who signed the DMK agreement document—the first victory on the question of Dravida Nadu—

<!-- Tamil source: scan 62 / printed 60 -->

! If those who come driving cattle ascend the throne again, it means our question is postponed for a little while!

Once our voice rises in the Legislative Assembly and the People's Assembly, those who came driving cattle will be frightened.

To create that fright, we must make those who come driving cattle in the election show us their backs.

If our voice rises in the Legislative Assembly and the People's Assembly, it means victory for our agreement document!

The cry that rises in those assemblies must make Delhi run toward Kanchipuram.

To hear that happy news, defeat the cattle-drivers!

Congressmen must hang the festoons of defeat! For Dravida to separate—for support to be won for the Dravidian question—make the cattle-drivers bite the dust!

Victory to the Dravidians! Long live Dravida!
