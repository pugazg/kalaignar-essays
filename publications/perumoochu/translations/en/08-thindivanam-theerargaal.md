# O Heroes of Tindivanam!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 49 / printed 47 -->

Koyanga is trying to come across our struggle for a separate Dravidian state and plant his flag in its path! A northern magnate; a representative of the deceivers who set up moneylending shops and turned fertile Tamil land into a parched land; an emissary of the class that, after the days when it lay at the Tamils' feet, now learns at the Tamils' feet only to bring Tamils low—he has entered the Tindivanam region and raised the battle cry against Tirukkural Munisami, at a time when the strong shoulders of Dravidian warriors have not yet worn thin in Dravida!

We see little jackals wielding the sceptre in the lion's cave. At a time when many valiant Dravidians are labouring to put an end to that cowardice, Koyanga has made his entrance.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 50 / printed 48 -->

If one of the commanders of Dravida's permanent enemies, who wear Dravida down through *Dinamani*, now seeks to beat the drum of victory and sing a heroic ode in Tindivanam, what greater hour of trial need come to the warriors?

In ancient days a hero placed stones on the heads of the northern Kanaka-Visayars who had insulted the Dravidians and dragged them back. Now that lineage of Kanaka-Visayars comes toward Dravida to defeat the lineage of the Cheras.

The strength of their own newspaper! The propaganda of newspaper employees who work ceaselessly all around! Marwaris who, suspecting those very propagandists, hide behind them and spy upon them as they work! This is how the election battle proceeds in Tindivanam!

Those in the Tindivanam constituency are not fools! They have not forgotten the revolutionary song: “Will lion-bulls sleep after giving everything for rats to devour?”

<!-- Tamil source: scan 51 / printed 49 -->

The Tindivanam constituency must not forget that every vote cast for Koyanga is an explosive blast delivered against Dravidian life!

The mill-kings are in the North! Political leadership is in the North! The sceptre of government is in the North! Holding all these grips, the North that gives no honour to national Chidambaram who pulled the oil-press, the North that does not even think of Tiruppur Kumaran, the North that sings no praise of Thillaiyadi Valliammai, the North that exploits the South, the North that lives by trampling Dravida—this North has decided to field a candidate in Dravida itself and weigh the measure of Dravida's gullibility!

Tirukkural Munisami has the support of the DMK. He cares about the question of a separate Dravidian state! His victory is the victory of the Dravida Nadu question! The victory of the DMK's Madurai resolution!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 52 / printed 50 -->

If Koyanga falls, the Tindivanam constituency, for as long as Dravida endures, will receive the sacred name “Grove of Heroes”! If Koyanga wins, Tindivanam will incur the disgraceful charge that Vibhishanas roam there!

Voters of the Tindivanam constituency! Is your support for Tirukkural Munisami, who supports the Dravidian question? Or for Koyanga, the great bandicoot of northern imperialism? Voters of Tindivanam! Is your support for the lineage of Cheran Senguttuvan? Or for the clan of Kanaka Visayan who comes to bring disgrace?

Pure Dravidians in whose blood self-respect courses! Speak your answer! Make the scheming North lose heart! Long live Dravidian honour!
