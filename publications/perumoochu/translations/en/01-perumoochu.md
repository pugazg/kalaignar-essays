# A Deep Sigh

<!-- Tamil source: scan 7 / printed 5 -->

The radiance that rises on the face of the man who toiled, dug down into the depths, and brought up pure gold is richer in quality than the gleam that rises from the gold itself.

The joy that blossoms in the eyes of the man who held his breath and brought up a pearl is of a higher order than the pearl's own lustre.

The cool delight that glides through the heart of the maiden who has walked through a forest of sharp thorns and plucked the rose blooming among the thorns that scratched her is greater than the coolness of the flower itself. For months they toiled; for days they waited in expectation; they stood against the jeers and ridicule of opponents, against those who sneered, "A conference, is it?" and spoke words of ill omen; they brushed them aside—crossed the hardships that came raging at them—while the fear of how it could possibly be conducted shook them, while the doubt whether it would go well clawed at their hearts. With dignity alone as their shield, and discipline and the movement's ideals as their weapons, they took up the task of conducting the great State Conference—

<!-- Tamil source: scan 8 / printed 6 -->

—and the heroes who brought it off now stand with heads held high, letting out a deep—but joyous—sigh.

To all of them we offer our greetings as a tribute.

How many ideas leap and play within the deep sigh breathed by those heroes! How many songs of delight pour from it! How many dances of awakening beat the drum within it!

On a scale never before seen in the political history of the South, the procession of the victorious heroes moved: the flags of more than six hundred branch units whirled and flew like ceremonial fans; a decorated elephant swayed forward bearing the flag of the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam; champions of the cause kept watch from horseback; ranks of younger brothers came around them; comrades from the branch organisations marched district by district; garlands were showered like flowers; all along the route, people stood in row after row with folded hands, offering greetings. With hearts swelling and smiles playing on their faces, the procession stretched five miles in distance and two miles in length. That deep sigh proclaims it with pride!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 9 / printed 7 -->

The new method the movement has devised for the propagation of ideas—an art exhibition extending for a furlong—stood up as an example of constructive work, scattering social, economic, political, scientific and philosophical thought in abundance and holding out heaps upon heaps of ideas by the thousands. That deep sigh thunders it forth!

A four-day conference! Night and day; drama—speech—more than two hundred thousand people. Lack of facilities—lack of space—amid all these obstacles, the young bulls who never lost their ardour, the skill with which they welcomed and served all those people: that deep sigh makes all this plain! The front-line heroes sang battle songs in a manner that recalled the warriors of the Chera, Chola and Pandya realms; the two hundred thousand who listened merged completely into a flood of collective feeling; that deep sigh turns that sight beyond seeing into poetry!

"O teardrops! O precious ones of the nation!" When warrior Anna cast out those trembling words in a voice choked with emotion, the younger brothers on the stage were moved. Those in the pavilion shivered to the core.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 10 / printed 8 -->

That deep sigh turns that scene into an epic!

Those who saw it heave a deep sigh. Those who did not see it hear the account of those who did—and they too heave a deep sigh.

A great conference of achievement such as the political history of the South has never seen! A conference that was the fruit of many days of labour!

A conference that was the reward received by those who toiled!

That is why those who conducted the conference—and those who saw the conference—breathe a deep sigh of joy. Let that deep sigh become the foundation for the breaths of feeling that are yet to come! That breath is not the result of sorrow! Not the shadow of patience! It is the roar of fulfilment! The sign of exultation that says: we finished the task we undertook; the great drum that sounds out that many more tasks remain to be undertaken!
