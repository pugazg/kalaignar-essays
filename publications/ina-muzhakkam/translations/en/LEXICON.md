## Frozen Tamil anomaly/conflict rule

Translation always follows the frozen Tamil authority, not a scan alternative. The following body forms are explicitly source-sensitive because P5 recorded a scan/baseline disagreement:

- `பாரட்டத்`
- `ஜோதில்கலந்தான்`
- `மனமுள்ளார்`
- `ஏறிபத்தர்`
- `முடுக்கினாள்`
- `போர் வீரனாம்`
- `கதறினாள்`

Do not replace these with scan-appearing alternatives during English translation. Translate the meaning supported by the frozen Tamil context and record any material English consequence in `TRANSLATION_REVIEW.md`.

## Poetry rule

For `கவிதைகள்`:

- keep each source-visible poem heading as a distinct heading;
- retain source line divisions where practical;
- preserve parallelism, address, commands, questions and exclamations;
- prioritise semantic/rhetorical fidelity over rhyme;
- do not invent imagery, metre or connective prose.
