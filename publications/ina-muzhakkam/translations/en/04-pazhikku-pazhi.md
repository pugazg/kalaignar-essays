# Revenge for Revenge

<!-- Tamil source: scan 30 / printed numeral not visible -->

“If our country is to prosper, the communal hatreds in the country must be rooted out entirely; the eradication of communalism that could not achieve complete success during the Mahatma's lifetime must at least be achieved as a result of his death ........”

This is not a resolution of the Dravidar Kazhagam! It is the substance of a decision of the Congress Working Committee.

We welcome it; we take pride in saying, ‘We said it; now they say it.’ We rejoice that this is the echo of our camp's anthem.

When we said that the demon called religion must not be allowed to possess us, what we received was the label of atheist. Today, for this thought to enter their minds, Gandhiyar had to be sacrificed.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 31 / printed 30 -->

Had this clarity and this decision become action earlier, the horrific murder of a noble man would not have taken place.

Let us forget what has happened. With our eyes fixed on the country's welfare........ let us at least from now on walk the good path........ That is the pledge the Dravidians need now!

“Down with communalism,” says the Working Committee.

The inquiry, ‘What is that accursed communalism?........ Where is the “lifeline” of that communalism......?’ is absolutely necessary.

The Puranic preachers say, ‘If you enter through the lotus stalk and go to the underworld...... Mayil Ravanan's life is in the great beetle flying in a crevice among the rocks there.’

The lifeline of communalism, too, has been arranged in just that way. If communalism is to fall, superficial reforms are of no use! Its

<!-- Tamil source: scan 32 / printed 31 -->

taproot must be cut. To allow communal divisions and yet think of destroying communalism is impossible! It is like lighting a fire and then searching for water to put it out!

When the communal division disappears, the doctrine disappears—when the communal division grows, communalism grows.

Our enemies propagate that when we say Brahminism must not be—must not be allowed—it is communalism. Is the instrument that incites communal riots called communalism? Or are the Dravidar Kazhagam policies that smash communal discrimination called communalism?

The cause of riots and struggles is Brahminism, stubbornly clinging to its inhuman doctrines and displaying caste superiority in this country—in agraharams—in temples—in eating houses—in the political sphere—and the Brahmin Seva Associations; they must be treated as communal organizations and abolished.

If communal divisions—caste systems—high and low status by reason of birth are abolished,

<!-- Tamil source: scan 33 / printed 32 -->

the distinctions of Paraiyan—Pallan—Brahmin will disappear. One association for each caste—competition among associations......... that condition will change. Equality will bloom. Samadharma will flourish.

This alone is the true memorial to be built for Gandhiyar! The right way for communalism to fall...... is to swing the sword at the lifeline of communalism! Our rulers must do this.

‘Organizations based on religion must not be allowed to work with political aims.’

This resolution of the Congress Working Committee, too, is greatly worthy of praise. Our view is that not merely should organizations based on religion be barred from working with political aims—the very existence of organizations based on religion is dangerous—a warning sign—it will become the cause for another Gotse to arise.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 34 / printed 33 -->

It cannot be said that distortions of this kind arise only because religious organizations work with political aims.......

The agitation, ‘What devotion can a Paraiyan have?’ pushed Nandan into the fire and said, ‘He merged with the Jothi.’ Behind the screen of religion, that enormous conspiracy was hidden.

Ramalingar, who said, ‘Let the demon called religion vanish,’ was murdered by religious fanatics. The colossal lie, ‘He became one with the Arut Sothi,’ was created. Religious devotion veiled the people's eyes—their thought.

Thirugnanasambandar had eight thousand Jains impaled........... and gave the impaling stakes a ‘blood abhishekam.’ The people did not ask why. Religious faith made them mute.

Even now, what if Gandhiyar, instead of being shot in front of the public in New Delhi, had been killed at Wardha Ashram? Those very killers would have

<!-- Tamil source: scan 35 / printed 34 -->

taken him away, buried him, and sung a Purana saying, “Gandhiji suddenly boarded a Pushpa Vimanam and went to the heavenly realm.” The conspiracy wrought by religious fanaticism would have been concealed.

Just as this conspiratorial religion has until now lived a life of pleasure by destroying Nandan—cutting short Vallalar's life—killing the Jains, the way would have been cleared for it to live on in the same manner.

But Gandhiyar's assassination has photographed and shown us all—no, shown all those who refuse to listen to us—the blood-soaked tongue of the Hindu religion.

That is why we say: let the valiant Gandhi be the last victim of religious fanaticism!

Gandhiyar's tragic end is the story of pouring milk for a snake! The very Varnashrama he worked to protect became the snake that drank his life! The very Hindu religious reform for which he worked...... that same Hindu religion, in the end, gave him three

<!-- Tamil source: scan 36 / printed 35 -->

bullets as its gift. Gandhiyar laboured for ahimsa...... Gotse's cruel grin is proof that he did not succeed in it!

Gandhiyar preached truth—the path of satya—these things. The newspapers of the Hindu religion that distort the news and incite lawlessness are proof that he failed in that!

Gandhiyar laboured for Hindu-Muslim unity—there too he had no success; Pakistan being divided off is proof!

Gandhiyar paved the way for peace...... that he found no consolation there either is proved by the great riots in the country!

What was it that stood as a stumbling block on Gandhiyar's paths of victory?....... What? What?......... Religion! Religious fanaticism! This frenzy felled a freedom fighter...... Should we not take revenge on this religion—revenge for

<!-- Tamil source: scan 37 / printed 36 -->

revenge? Should we not at least save the leaders who remain? Dravidians? Tell me!
