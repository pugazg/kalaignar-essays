## Quotations, verse and literary language

This collection contains highly literary prose and may contain verse-like lineation, metaphor, personification, dialogue, direct address and rhetorical repetition.

- preserve source quotation status and speaker boundaries;
- preserve verse/line divisions where the source presents them as verse;
- do not invent rhyme;
- preserve direct address, rhetorical questions, commands, repetitions, exclamations and abrupt contrasts;
- retain metaphor and personification rather than replacing them with explanatory abstraction;
- do not silently identify unexplained literary/cultural references from memory or another edition;
- document materially difficult choices in `translations/en/TRANSLATION_REVIEW.md` and recurring decisions in `LEXICON.md`.
