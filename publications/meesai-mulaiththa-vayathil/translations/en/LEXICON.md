## E1/E2 voice and source discipline

- compact literary compounds may remain compact when intelligible;
- direct apostrophe/personification is retained rather than explained away;
- deliberate repetitions, reversals, sarcasm, commands and harsh formulations remain;
- source-specific names, labels and puns are not expanded from outside knowledge;
- culturally specific forms are transliterated only when that preserves source function better than an invented English equivalent;
- source irregularities that affect interpretation belong in `TRANSLATION_REVIEW.md`, not in silent body repair.

## Quotations / verse / title policy

- A published or web English translation of quoted material is not imported unless explicitly authorized.
- Verse or verse-like lineation is translated semantically/rhetorically; rhyme is never invented merely for polish.
- English article titles are established article by article.
- Strange or compressed source imagery is not normalized into explanatory prose solely because literal English sounds unusual.
