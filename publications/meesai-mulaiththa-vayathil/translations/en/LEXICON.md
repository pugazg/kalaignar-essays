Record especially:

- recurring metaphors and personifications;
- direct-address forms;
- ideologically, socially or culturally loaded vocabulary;
- difficult compounds;
- epithets and labels whose force could be softened in English;
- source-specific puns, coined labels or unexplained references;
- publication/source labels;
- title renderings when they establish a reusable pattern;
- intentional contextual variants.

For every entry:

1. determine the function in the immediate Tamil sentence;
2. preserve rhetorical force rather than choosing a smoother neutral synonym;
3. distinguish a source-bearing transliteration from an explanatory note;
4. allow contextual variation when the Tamil meaning genuinely changes;
5. do not invent a stronger social, caste, religious, political or historical identification than the source supports.
