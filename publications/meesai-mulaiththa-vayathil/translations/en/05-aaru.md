# River

<!-- Tamil source: scan 31 / printed 30 -->

## Beauty

O river! O dancing woman! Your beauty, curving like the expressive hand of a dancer, is beauty indeed! O swelling flood of foam! Can even the light of molten silver equal you! With a sound like anklets setting melody, a gait gathering every colour, and a form that fills every farmer's thought with delight—Ponni, queen among women! Live on, live on!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 32 / printed 31 -->

## Beloved

O woman of love! O eye of the Kaveri! O lady with coral lips brushed in red! In love overflowing with delight, is the alluvium you leave behind the betel you have spat into the mouth of the handsome Earth?

## Mother

O mother who stretches out an unfailing hand to raise children whose baby speech is not yet formed! See the red-paddy children, their bodies of burnished gold swaying, smiling with jewel-like teeth beneath the embrace of your loving arm! You bring them nature's joy! Precious mother! Mother River! Knowingly or unknowingly, you give milk even to evil weeds. Did you learn this fault from your mother? Is it a cradle-habit—or did that pure one learn it from you!

## Government

Who can equal you in setting good things out in festive display and rejoicing over them? The yellow grain-ear lies in your lap and raises its head. You wet the feet of the sugarcane and load every joint with sweetness. Banana—mango—jackfruit—and all else needed for life—conceive only after your touch. I would call you a government that labours only to see the common good! You enter the country singing welfare: a good government! O river-government, moving toward the sea while doing your duty! Do not imagine merely that you go and fill the sea! That is exactly what those who govern in this world imagine. Because the sea-water became cloud, you run as a sandal-coloured river! Government is like the river—the creation of the ocean of the people! If you lose your course and run wild, they will build a dam and stop you, O river!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 33 / printed 32 -->

The government that rules a country meets the same fate!

As you run, keep teaching this lesson—

Live on, O river! Live on! O river!
