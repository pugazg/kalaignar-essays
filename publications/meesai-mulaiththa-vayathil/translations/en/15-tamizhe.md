# O Tamil!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 62 / printed 61 -->

O Tamil!

You—I feel like calling you: O painting that captures the heart! Epic of exhilaration! Fragrant flower of the stream! Radiance-pouring new moon! Love! Beauty! Nectar! Life! Delight! Sweet breeze! Dew! Fruit! Green-plumed peacock! Taste of fruit-juice! Emerald gem! Ruby flame! Lamp of humankind!—and so much more.

Yet, O Tamil! I would say that the joy in calling you simply “Tamil!” is found in no other word.
