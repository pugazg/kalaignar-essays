# Fame

<!-- Tamil source: scan 57 / printed 56 -->

Fame! You are a courtesan. From those who wander until their legs ache in search of you, you take money and give love.

Fame! You are a lunar realm. In their attempt to reach you, many great minds have still not succeeded.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 58 / printed 57 -->

Fame! You are the summit of the Himalaya. Through unceasing effort, some will seize you.

Fame! You are a block of ice. Even if one keeps you in the palm and grips you tightly, you melt into water and disappear.

Fame! You are a jar of liquor. The flies that fall into you have never risen again. Fame! You are a shadow! You will keep following those who do not worry about you.

Fame! You are a riddle! I cannot even explain you!
