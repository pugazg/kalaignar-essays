# O Crescent!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 18 / printed 17 -->

O crescent! Foam of the wave of the sky-sea!  
Why do you peer into the prison through the window and laugh?

I hear you calling me “Prisoner!” And what of you? Do you roam about mounted on the chariot of freedom? Without crossing the line drawn for you, without closing your wakeful eye, you have only the right to keep walking like a prison guard.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 19 / printed 18 -->

I cannot leap to the beloved who stands pining outside the prison walls. Some life you have! You too cannot stretch out your hand and touch the star-ladies winking beyond your boundary!

I too am a prisoner!

You too are a prisoner!

In the outside world I have my blackouts. You have yours too: once a month—the new moon! They will not let me shine as I should—they hide my full light by trickery. Some life you have! Clouds around you—enemies around me.

I too am a prisoner!

You too are a prisoner!

I, at least, will gain freedom—along with my country. For you there is no freedom at all—no change at all in your circling life.

O crescent! Boat of the sky! Carry my white-plumed peacock here for a little while. Bring me my fair one who lies there yearning, thinking of me. I gaze at her through the window and am transported! Bring me my goddess of freedom.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 20 / printed 19 -->

Here I am asking you—but how can you? You cannot cross the line drawn for you!

You too are a prisoner!

I too am a prisoner!
