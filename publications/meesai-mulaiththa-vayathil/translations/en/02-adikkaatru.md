# Aadi Wind

<!-- Tamil source: scan 21 / printed 20 -->

O raging Aadi wind! Whom are you trying to frighten? Is your fury greater than the fury of those seated on thrones! I am one who has endured that very fury. They decreed that kith, kin, wife, children, mother, father and friends—none of them—may stay with me; what is the secret by which they have let only you inside!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 22 / printed 21 -->

Is it because I cannot see their frenzied durbar outside that they are showing me that durbar in your form!

O Aadi wind! Why do you toss the trees about like this? Why do you make them dance as a sorcerer makes a spirit dance? Why do you make them dance as ministers make the people dance? Why do you torment and torture them? What crime have those trees committed? Was it wrong to flourish? Wrong to give abundance? Wrong to enjoy a renaissance? Was it wrong that, wearing fragrant flowers in their hair-knots, they stood there like enchantresses of beauty? What was wrong? Why do you punish them? O Aadi wind! Tell me the reason! You will not. The government, which is like you, gave us no reason either! O Aadi wind! If you scoop up earth and fling it into our eyes, do you think we will be afraid? Are the grains of sand your hands shower upon us harsher than a tear-gas attack! O Aadi wind, leaping like a monkey after crossing the wall! Even after I have come to prison, must you still try to torture me? What—without answering, you only breathe a dreadful breath?

Dictators too made a ghastly roar like you; they too staged a frenzy of lawlessness. You and they are of one breed. O Aadi wind, singing that truth in a howling tone—

<!-- Tamil source: scan 23 / printed 22 -->

wait! Wait! Once Aadi has run its course, you will subside. After you have subsided, you will go to meet your government friend who strutted about just like you—your intimate companion! Ayyo, poor fellow; by then he will have gone into the hole before you!

Go—go, scoop up the earth  
and scatter it as you go!
