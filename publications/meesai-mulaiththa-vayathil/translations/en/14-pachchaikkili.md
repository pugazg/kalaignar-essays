# Green Parrot

<!-- Tamil source: scan 59 / printed 58 -->

Take the tender head of a bottle gourd,  
set a red chilli upon it,  
put jasmine leaves at the sides as wings  
and a screw-pine frond behind—  
O green parrot!  
a creation like you! a creation like you!  
Round head! Red beak!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 60 / printed 59 -->

Little wings! Long tail!  
Did the cheeky parrot speak amiss?  
Asked by the sky, you refuse to bless.  
You will not praise one who asks for praise.  
I know the reason, little parrot!  
Because they put you in a cage,  
you too writhe like a worm on a hook.  
I rejoiced in shutting you in prison;  
today I have learned:  
prison is cruel indeed! prison is cruel indeed!  
Forgive me, forgive me!  
O doll of golden speech, forgive me!  
When my prison ends, I shall break your prison!  
Through prison I learned the pain of prison.  
Enslaved bird! Lovely little parrot!  
I have learned by experience—I have learned by experience.  
When I return, I shall give you a life of freedom.  
Fly, fly, fly high!  
The instant your bound feet are freed,  
fly upward as far as you can reach!  
O brightly coloured maiden, fly everywhere!  
I shut you in a cage, parrot!  
Forgive me! I have understood  
the pain of the cage. Because I have understood  
the pain of the cage, I shall open  
your cage and set you free.

<!-- Tamil source: scan 61 / printed 60 -->

But, parrot—  
those here who understood the pain of the cage  
came to rule;  
they put us in a cage to die.  
Why, parrot? Why, parrot?  
Tell me the answer too, green parrot!  
“You imprisoned me; therefore they imprisoned you,”  
you will say. Good, parrot; then—  
whom had you imprisoned  
that made me imprison you?
