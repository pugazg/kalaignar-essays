This is the publication-specific living terminology / source-bearing decisions ledger for the English layer.

Permanent policy: [`../../../../ESSAY_TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md`](../../../../ESSAY_TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md)

> **Translate the language; do not neutralise the voice.**

## Rules

1. Translate from the frozen strict-reviewed Tamil assemblies only.
2. Preserve Kalaignar's source label, rhetorical function, sarcasm and polemical force.
3. Do not silently identify unexplained mock labels, coined forms or source-specific references from memory or outside knowledge.
4. Contextual variants are allowed when the Tamil grammatical/rhetorical role genuinely changes; record them here.
5. Do not use the P5 scan/baseline conflict ledger to “repair” the frozen Tamil in English.
6. Physical-source gaps remain gaps; English must not manufacture hidden wording.
7. T1 decisions are working translation choices until the relevant review gates establish them. Forms marked T5 VERIFIED are frozen for that verified article unless a concrete source-supported defect reopens the gate.

## Article 2 T3 voice decisions

T3 preserved every T2-secured semantic/source distinction and made only cadence/directness changes:

- **`All who come and go look at him as they pass.`** keeps the opening movement compact.
- **`He does wear the sacred thread.`** and **`The thread is only hidden beneath his shirt—that is all.`** sharpen the source's pointed aside without adding an identification.
- **`To those coming toward the beach...`** and **`And he doesn't stop there.`** restore the direct forward motion of the paragraph.
- **`An Ayyar accepting Dravidam? You will be startled.`** puts the source's startled challenge back in question-first order while retaining the same claim.
- The fire-stepping ridicule is tightened to **`at the very word Dravidam, leaps as though he has stepped on fire`**, and the ending to **`went away, head bowed`**.
- The final imperative sequence now lands as **`to go and see. And along with that, read carefully what follows below.`**, preserving the commands while avoiding a softened explanatory flow.
