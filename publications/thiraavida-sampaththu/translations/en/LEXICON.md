Permanent policy: [`../../../../ESSAY_TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md`](../../../../ESSAY_TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md)

> **Translate the language; do not neutralise the voice.**

## Rules

1. Translate from the frozen strict-reviewed Tamil assemblies only.
2. Preserve Kalaignar's source label, rhetorical function, sarcasm and polemical force.
3. Do not silently identify unexplained mock labels, coined forms or source-specific references from memory or outside knowledge.
4. Contextual variants are allowed when the Tamil grammatical/rhetorical role genuinely changes; record them here.
5. Do not use the P5 scan/baseline conflict ledger to “repair” the frozen Tamil in English.
6. Physical-source gaps remain gaps; English must not manufacture hidden wording.
7. T1 decisions are working translation choices unless marked established; T2–T4 may refine them for fidelity/voice/terminology without reopening the frozen Tamil source.

## Article 1 T2 source-damage decisions

The damaged scan-15 quotation and scan-16 ending were reviewed against the frozen Tamil assembly. T2 tightened the English so that it does not silently complete visibly broken Tamil forms:

- scan 15 `சம்பத்து என்றா` is rendered **`Wealth mea—`**, not the grammatically completed `Wealth means`;
- the damaged continuation beginning with surviving `ழி வழங்கும் பிரதேசத்...` is represented as **`...[damaged fragment] in the region where it prevails`**, avoiding insertion of a reconstructed hidden noun;
- surviving `பாது` remains **`protec—`**;
- surviving `தக்க பெருஞ்செல்வம்` remains **`...a great treasure worthy...`**;
- scan 16 `கத்` remains an incomplete **`shou—`** fragment;
- no hidden wording under torn paper was supplied.

## Other Article 1 T2 fidelity corrections

- source masculine personification `பைத்தியக்காரனல்லவே ... கக்கியிருக்கிறான்` is now reflected as **`isn't such a madman ... he ... hasn't he spat... ?`** rather than neuter `it`;
- `விகடர்`-family forms are retained as **Vikatar**, distinct from publication-name **Ananda Vikatan**;
- language-geography `வழங்கிய` is now **prevailed**, while later `பேசியவர்கள்` remains **those who spoke**;
- no title or mock-label choice was release-frozen at T2.
