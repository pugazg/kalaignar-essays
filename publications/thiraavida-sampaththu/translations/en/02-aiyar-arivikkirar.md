# Iyer Announces!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 12 -->

There stands that Iyer on Chennai's seashore. Everyone who goes and comes looks at him and passes on. He is indeed one who wears the sacred thread. The thread is simply hidden under the shirt he has on, that is all. Looking at those who come toward the beach, he says, "Countrymen; countrymen, a message." He does not stop there. "There is a word called Dravidam; it is sweet-tasting honey, language sugar-candy," he sings. You will be astonished.

You will be startled: an Ayyar accepting Dravidam? True, friends, true! One day, my friends and I met that Ayyar on the beach. He sang the song quoted above. He cried, 'Dravidam, Dravidam'. Even a member of the "Enothano" party—one who, at the mere word Dravidam, jumps as though he has stepped on fire—heard that Ayyar's cry and went away with his head bowed.

Go at once to Chennai's seashore. At the gate of Presidency College stands

<!-- Tamil source: scan 3 -->

that ayam. You will know whether what I said is true or not. Once you know—

then tell those Enothanos—those things that lay somewhere—those place-seekers—to go and see; and along with that, read carefully what follows below.

The words written beneath the statue of Dr. U. Ve. Saminathayyar at the gate of Presidency kalluri on Chennai's seashore:

"Maha Mahopadhyaya Dravida Vidya Bhushana Dr. Saminatha Ayyar"—those are the words!
