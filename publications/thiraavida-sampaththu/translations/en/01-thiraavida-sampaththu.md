# Dravidian Wealth

<!-- Tamil source: scan 5 -->

*

## Political Patients

They lie there, eyes shut. Suddenly they cry out, 'Oh, father!' There are some political patients like this. I call them patients; they call it a 'stunt'—mouth shut, tail tucked in. Suddenly getting up and pouncing. If the pounce pays, they leap farther and farther; if not, they hang from the tree branch as before. This is a familiar affair for Ananda Vikatan, the representative of our agraharam—the beloved child of the ammamis.

## The Tail Is Wagging

It began writing about the wealth of the South and has

<!-- Tamil source: scan 6 -->

pounced at us. The title: Dravidian Wealth!—and in it Vikatan's tail is wagging strangely—oddly—perversely,

"It would not be wrong to infer that the story of Mahishasura Mardini was an old Dravidian story into which mythology entered."

That is how it begins. I was astonished. I turned it over and over to see whether what we were reading really was Ananda Vikatan.

'Mythology has entered Dravidian stories'—is that an ordinary thing to say? And that too Vikatan, the sole voice of the Vedic savants—would it write like this? It has written it. So I was amazed,

'Mythology has entered Dravidian stories'—what a marvellous truth! How much we have suffered for saying this. We have.

## Mythology Has Entered

Mythology has entered Dravidian stories. Yes; the Ramayana is a Dravidian story, a Dravidian story that arose about the war between Rama and Ravana, and we said that mythology entered it, we

<!-- Tamil source: scan 13 -->

said. Those who opposed it ...; now Vikatan says... that mythology entered the Dravidian story called Mahishasura Mardini!

We give thanks—wisdom has dawned, even if late! But Vikatan isn't such a madman after all......he has not completely abandoned his old habit; at the end of the article, hasn't he spat out a little poison?

## Poisonous Work

Afraid that, by calling it a Dravidian story, it might give room to the 'Dravida Nadu!' question, it says: "By Dravidian Wealth, we do not accept that such a race ever existed or exists."

Look at Vikatan's poison. "The doctrine of racial hatred is a mit (Myth), that is, a mythological imagination, say today's scientific scholars and researchers."

Look what he uses scientific scholars and researchers for, Vikatanar! The scholars, it seems, say the doctrine of racial hatred is wrong. With these words, the Comedy Monkey dreams of ruining our racial clarion call!

<!-- Tamil source: scan 14 -->

## Racial Hatred—Attachment to One's Race

Racial hatred is indeed wrong. That is exactly what we keep pointing out to the Aryan race. We say: give up the arrogance that a human being must not be touched—the thick-headed conceit that you alone must worship Maheswaran—give up racial hatred. What we have is not racial hatred but attachment to our race. Racial hatred...destroying and suppressing another race and developing one's own. Attachment to one's race..protecting one's own race and creating a racial awakening.

The former is the Aryan way. The latter is the Dravidian way. Our way.

Did the Aryan Anjaneyar who meant to make mischief at least manage to write without contradicting himself from beginning to end? Not even that.

## The Wedge-Meddling Monkey

First he spoke in our voice, saying that mythology had entered a Dravidian story.

Then he roared in his own language that the doctrine of racial hatred must not exist.

## Cuckoos of the Garbage Heap

The place where the English language prevailed is England; the country where the French language prevailed is France. Why, then, is it that Ananda Vikatar does not know that the country where the Dravidian language prevailed is Dravida Nadu? It isn't that he doesn't know. He knows—and still has a little courage left to conceal it!

Let us leave aside region and country. Those who spoke the Dravidian language must have been Dravidians, mustn't they, Vikatar? Just as those who spoke Greek were Greeks, Vikatar! Poisoning truths through malice is your hereditary occupation. I certainly don't imagine that, merely because of the explanation I've given, you'll climb down from the tree tomorrow itself.
