## Source-irregularity handling

Two Article-1 forms remain intentionally exposed rather than silently regularised:

1. scan 29 frozen `அனுமதியை` — body renders **permission** and carries a separated translator note; it is not silently changed to `அமைதியை`;
2. scan 47 frozen `இரண்டாமிரம்` — translator note records the printed form while the immediately repeated numerical sense is rendered **two thousand**.

These notes do not alter the frozen Tamil source.

## E6 cross-article decision

The cross-article review found **no terminology change requiring an English body edit**.

Intentional contextual variation retained:

- **Dravida / Dravida land / Dravidians / Dravidian** according to grammatical and source function;
- **liberation / freedom** and **uprising / struggle** only where the immediate Tamil warrants a contextual variant;
- period and source-bearing forms are not modernised for uniformity.

## Rhetorical / quotation policy

- retain direct questions, exclamations, repeated clauses, slogans, parallel lists and abrupt contrasts;
- do not soften insults, ridicule, accusations or polemical labels;
- do not convert the prose into detached historical narration;
- keep quotations as quotations and preserve attribution/order;
- political and historical statements are translations of the frozen 1953 source, not independently verified present-day claims;
- do not add present-day geopolitical, electoral or ideological commentary to the translated body.
